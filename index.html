WTFP

Water the F**king Plants — an iPhone app that nags you, affectionately, to water your houseplants.

Add each plant once and say how thirsty it is. WTFP works out when each one is next due, sends one reminder a day, and lets you mark a plant watered straight from the notification or the home-screen widget. Miss a few days? Fine. The schedule follows when you actually water, not a calendar.

No account. No cloud. No nonsense — everything stays on your phone.

Support

Something broken, or a plant-related grievance? Email c.mindiashvili@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if it's a bug.

Frequently asked

Why is a new plant "due now"? Because adding a plant means you want to look after it, not that you've just watered it. Water it once and the clock starts.

I marked something watered by mistake. Tap Undo on the bar that appears, or open the plant and choose Undo last watering from the ••• menu. You can also swipe away any history entry.

Where's my data? On your phone, and nowhere else. Settings → Export everything gives you a file you can keep or move to a new phone.
