Privacy policy

Effective 17 September 2026 · WTFP (Water the F**king Plants) for iOS

The short version: WTFP collects nothing. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Your plants, rooms, care schedules, watering history, notes and photos are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. The app's widget and notification actions read that same on-device storage. Nothing is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • No data is sent to any server operated by the developer or by third parties.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or advertising frameworks are included.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no identifiers are collected.
  • No account is created and no personal information is requested.

Permissions

Notifications — optional. Used only to remind you when a plant is due. Reminders are scheduled locally on the device.
Camera and photo library — optional. Used only to attach a photo to a plant; the photo is stored on the device with the plant.

Backups and export

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted iTunes/Finder backup, the app's data is included in that backup under Apple's terms. The app's own Export feature writes a file to a location you choose; where that file goes afterwards is up to you.

Children

The app is rated 12+ on the App Store for infrequent mild language. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
